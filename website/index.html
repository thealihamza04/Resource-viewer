Questions

Is Resource Viewer free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads, accounts or upgrades.

Will cleaning delete my passwords or history?

No. Browser cleanup only removes the cache. Cookies, saved passwords, history and bookmarks are never touched.

Should I clear my RAM cache?

No. File cache is spare memory that makes files open faster, and your system hands it to apps the moment they need it.
